The Founder

Rachelle Pilares-Buenaventura

Rachelle's Sherbet & Ice Cream was created by Rachelle Pilares-Buenaventura, a self-taught confectioner from Pandi, Bulacan. What started as a passion for real, honest flavor grew into a small local business known for its traditionally made ice cream and sherbet.

Every batch leans on real fruits and natural ingredients rather than shortcuts — from the coconut in a buko sherbet to the roasted corn folded into corn & cheese ice cream. It's the kind of creaminess and true flavor that comes from doing things the traditional way.

What We Believe

Real goodness, in every serving

🍓 Real Fruits

Whole fruits and natural produce go into every sherbet and ice cream base — not extracts or artificial flavoring.

🌿 Natural Ingredients

Simple, honest ingredients so the true flavor of each fruit or add-in comes through in every scoop.

🤲 Traditional Methods

Small-batch, handcrafted recipes rooted in traditional Filipino confectionery techniques.
