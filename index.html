Our Story

Made the traditional way, by hand

Rachelle's Sherbet & Ice Cream is a small local business known for traditionally made ice cream and sherbet, crafted from real fruits and natural ingredients — no shortcuts, just true flavor in every scoop. It was created by Rachelle Pilares-Buenaventura, a self-taught confectioner from Pandi, Bulacan.
